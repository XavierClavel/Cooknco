# Play listing — English (en-GB / en-US)

Draft. Paste into Play Console → **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**, English.
Nothing here reaches the store on its own — see `store/README.md`.

Every claim below is a feature the app actually ships today. If one stops being true, it
comes out of here before the next listing edit: a screenshot or a sentence describing
something the app no longer does is the kind of thing Play removes a listing over.

## Full description

4000 characters max. This draft is ~1650.

```
Cook&Co is a recipe book that belongs to you and the people you cook with.

Write a recipe once — ingredients, steps, a photo — and it is there on every device you sign
in on. No clipping, no fifteen paragraphs of preamble before the list of ingredients.

COOK FROM IT, NOT AROUND IT

Cook mode walks you through a recipe one step at a time, in type you can read from across
the counter. Steps that need a timer carry their own duration, so you start it with a tap
instead of reaching for another app — and it keeps running, with its own notification, while
you get on with the next thing.

Quantities scale to the number of servings you are actually making. Ingredients show in the
units you think in.

SHARE IT WITH THE PEOPLE YOU COOK WITH

Follow your friends and their new recipes turn up in your feed. Like the ones you want to
find again.

Cookbooks group recipes around whatever they have in common — a cuisine, a season, the
weeknight shortlist. Add friends to one and they can add their own recipes to it, so the
family's recipes end up in one place instead of in five phones.

TAKE IT OFF THE PHONE

Export a recipe, or an entire cookbook, as a clean printable PDF. For the folder in the
kitchen drawer, for the person who does not want an app, or for a gift.

EVERY RECIPE HAS A LINK

Recipes, cookbooks and profiles each have a web address that works whether or not the person
you send it to has the app. Tap one with the app installed and it opens there; tap it without
and it opens the same page in the browser.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE

The app is in English and French, and it writes to you in the language you chose rather than
whichever handset you last signed in on.

Cook&Co is free, has no advertising, and does not sell what you cook.
```

## Notes for whoever pastes this

- **The last line is a commitment, not copy.** It is true today. If advertising or any data
  sale is ever added, this line comes out in the same change — Play's Data safety section and
  the listing contradicting each other is a policy problem, not a wording one.
- **"Free, no advertising"** — keep consistent with the Data safety declaration and with
  whatever the app's own privacy copy says.
- The app has moderation tooling (`ui/moderation`) but it is not user-facing enough to
  advertise; left out on purpose.
- iOS is not shipped, so nothing here implies it.
