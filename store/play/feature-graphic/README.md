# Feature graphic — 1024×500

**Mandatory.** Play will not publish a listing without one, and it is what a listing card
uses wherever Play shows the app outside the listing itself.

- 1024×500 exactly, PNG or JPEG, **no alpha channel**
- No text near the edges: Play crops it differently across surfaces, and on some it overlays
  the app icon and title on the left
- Do not put the app name in it as the only place the name appears — it is decoration, the
  title comes from the listing

Ground colour to match the app: `#F0F4EF` (CookncoBackground). Accent: `#FF6F59`
(CookncoOrange). The mark is in `../icon/icon-fullbleed-1024.png`.

Name it `feature-graphic-1024x500-en.png` / `-fr.png` — Play takes one per locale, and the
French listing showing English words is the usual reason to want two.
