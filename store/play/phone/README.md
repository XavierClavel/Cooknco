# Phone screenshots

**2 minimum, 8 maximum.** Two is what Play needs to publish; the first two are the only ones
most people see, so lead with the screens that say what the app is.

- 16:9 or 9:16, each side between 320px and 3840px, PNG or JPEG
- The app as it actually looks. Not a device frame with marketing copy around it — Play
  rejects mockups that misrepresent the app
- Numbered, because the filename order is the display order

Suggested order, which follows the listing copy:

| File | Screen | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-recipe.png` | a recipe, ingredients and steps visible | says what the app is in one glance |
| `02-cook-mode.png` | cook mode mid-recipe, a step timer running | the thing no recipe website does |
| `03-feed.png` | the feed, friends' recipes | says it is shared, not a private notebook |
| `04-cookbook.png` | a cookbook with members | the feature people stay for |
| `05-edit.png` | writing a recipe | answers "how much work is this" |
| `06-pdf.png` | an exported PDF | the one people do not expect |

Capture on a real device or an emulator at a phone density, with plausible content — not
`Lorem ipsum`, and not the seed data's placeholder names.
